PULSEFALL is a top-down arena survival shooter: dodge, dash and blast escalating waves of geometric enemies, pick 1 of 3 upgrades between waves, and survive to wave 10 and beyond.

WASD or arrows to move, mouse to aim, click or hold to shoot, Shift or Space to dash through danger with a flash of invulnerability. Four enemy kinds hunt you in different ways — Chasers that pursue, Dashers that telegraph a lunge, Splitters that burst into minis, Weavers that strafe and shoot — and every 5th wave a Pulse Warden boss arrives with radial bullet rings. Chain kills to grow your combo multiplier, grab health and rapid-fire pickups, and build your ship wave by wave: damage, fire rate, split shot, shield, magnet, and more.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. No build step, no network requests — the game is plain static files under `game/`, all art drawn in code and all sound synthesized with Web Audio.
